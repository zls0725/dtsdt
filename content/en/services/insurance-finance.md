Protection first, then growth. Each plan is sized to the household, not to a product quota.

## 1. Needs analysis

A full look at finances, family and plans; then a risk map and a matching cover.

## 2. Product selection

Compare carriers, then a mix of life, health and accident cover that actually fits.

## 3. Wealth planning

Short-term cash products and a longer mix of equities, funds and property.

## 4. Claims support

Paperwork, process, and help if a claim is disputed.

The result should be cover you can use, plus a savings path that does not fight the insurance.
