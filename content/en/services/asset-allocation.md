Asset allocation is a full service for financial goals and risk control. It covers equities, bonds, funds, property and cash.

## 1. Equities

Market notes, stock selection matched to risk, and trade execution.

## 2. Bonds

Credit review, duration matching, and a mix of government and corporate paper.

## 3. Funds

Fund screening, periodic reviews, and rebalancing.

## 4. Property

Location research, holding structure, and cash-flow checks.

## 5. Cash and FX

Multi-currency cash, FX timing, and liquidity buffers.

The aim is a portfolio you can hold: diversified, reviewable, and adjusted as life changes.
