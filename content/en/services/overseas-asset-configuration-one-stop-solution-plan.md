Overseas work usually spans many vendors. Finding a new specialist for every new problem wastes time and stretches the timeline. We start from a service relationship: a plan based on your real situation, plus the issues you have not hit yet, then long-term follow-up.

## 1. Study and immigration

Country, city and school selection; applications, essays and interviews; student, work and investor visas; arrival support such as housing and language.

## 2. Education planning

Academic assessment, school and major selection, scholarships, and extra-curricular support.

## 3. Asset allocation

Financial review, a portfolio across equities, bonds and property, then regular review and rebalancing.

## 4. Insurance and wealth

Needs analysis, product selection, short- and long-term cash planning, and claims support.

## 5. Funds and securities

Fund selection, portfolio monitoring and market notes.

## 6. Family office

Trusts, succession, and family investment management so wealth transfers without leaking away.
