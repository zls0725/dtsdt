Family offices used to mean ultra-high-net-worth only. Mass-affluent households can still use the same toolkit: wealth planning, investment structure, tax, education, retirement, and risk control.

## Core work

- **Wealth management** — a household balance sheet, goals, and a review rhythm.
- **Investments** — allocation, manager selection, and reporting.
- **Tax and legal** — holding companies, trusts, and cross-border notes (with licensed counsel).
- **Education and retirement** — school funding and a drawdown plan.
- **Insurance** — a cover map that sits next to the investment book.

The point is coordination: one map for the family, not five vendors who never talk.
